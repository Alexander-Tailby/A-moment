A moment to notice.
A moment to act.

The animation shows a bleeding, or haemorrhagic, stroke. A stroke can also happen when a vessel is blocked. Both are medical emergencies.

F

Face

Has one side of their face drooped?

A

Arms

Can they raise both arms?

S

Speech

Is their speech slurred or hard to understand?

T

Time

Call emergency services immediately.

In Australia, call 000. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number.

Act even if symptoms disappear. F.A.S.T. covers common signs, but not all strokes.
